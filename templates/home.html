Student Performance Prediction and Feedback System

empowering students & faculty with AI-Driven insights

Our Student Performance Prediction and Feedback System is designed to support polytechnic faculty in understanding student progress more accurately. By analyzing internal or semester marks, the system predicts academic risk levels and generates personalized improvement suggestions that help students stay on track.

Why this system?

Polytechnic students often struggle to understand where they stand academically, and faculty spend a large amount of time manually reviewing individual results. This system bridges that gap by providing AI-powered analytics that highlight performance issues early and enable better academic planning.

What problem does it solve?
  • • Students don’t know which subjects need more attention
  • • Faculty require quick insights at class-level
  • • Tracking progress becomes difficult over multiple exams
  • • Feedback is often generalized rather than personalized
Core Features
🎯 Student Risk Prediction 📊 Faculty Dashboards 🧠 Personalized Feedback 📂 Bulk File Upload
Who benefits?

Faculty, students, and institutions — all gain from faster evaluation, targeted suggestions, and an organized academic monitoring method.

SPPFS brings clarity, prediction, and decision-making power into the academic process.
